# 🌍 2050: Letters from the Future

**Developed by TENSOR TITANS**

*The future is not written yet. Every choice matters.*

## About the Project

**2050: Letters from the Future** is an interactive environmental adventure game that challenges players to confront the consequences of environmental neglect and take action to create a better future.

Through letters from Jennifer, a girl living in 2050, players discover a world affected by river pollution, blocked drains, disappearing greenery, and unsustainable urban habits. By completing environmental missions, players help restore their surroundings and learn how individual and community choices can make a lasting difference.

### 🌱 Key Features
- Interactive environmental missions and meaningful player choices.
- River restoration and drain-cleaning challenges.
- Tree planting and environmental regeneration.
- Waste segregation and sustainable transportation.
- Personalised storytelling and a final environmental impact score.
- A narrative-driven experience connecting present-day actions to the future.

### 🌎 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals
- **SDG 6:** Clean Water and Sanitation
- **SDG 11:** Sustainable Cities and Communities
- **SDG 13:** Climate Action
